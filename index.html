Void

A browser that fades away.

One page, a few pixels of chrome around it, nothing else. Built on WebKit, the engine of macOS — no Chromium, no Electron.

  • ≈ 9MB, the whole app
  • 0external dependencies
  • MaciPhone and iPad too
  1. 01

    One page. Nothing else.

    The chrome stays out of the way: a sidebar, the page, and a new tab that asks nothing of you.

  2. 02

    Tabs on the side, or on top.

    Sidebar or top bar, your call. Switch without losing the page, mid-animation.

  3. 03

    Spaces that are truly separate.

    Personal, Work: each space has its own tabs and its own storage. Signed in here, not there. A two-finger swipe to move between them.

  4. 04

    The address bar does the math.

    “10 km in miles”, “20% of 150”, “√2”: the result shows up as you type. Everything is computed on your Mac, nothing is sent.

  5. 05

    Two pages side by side.

    ⌥⌘S brings the previous tab alongside. Click a page to give it focus.

  6. 06

    A tint of your own.

    Neutral, or one of eight pastel tints, with two soft glows. The frame turns slightly translucent.

  7. 07

    Your meeting follows you.

    During a Meet, Teams or Zoom call, leave the tab: the meeting moves into a small floating window, controls included.

Listen · ⇧⌘D

Listen, transcribe, summarize.

The microphone, your Mac's audio — a call, a video — or both. What's said is written sentence by sentence, voices are told apart, and Apple Intelligence writes a summary. On device: nothing you hear leaves.

  • Three tabs: Notes, Transcript, Summary
  • Recording kept, ≈ 10 MB per hour
  • “Me” and “Them”, or named voices

Lock · ⇧⌘L

Hidden instantly. Touch ID to open it again.

A locked note disappears without asking anything. To read it, Touch ID — or your Mac's password. The app never sees your fingerprint or your password.

What isn't there.

Void apps are defined above all by what they leave out.

No account

They work from the first launch. Nothing to create, nothing to confirm.

No server

Your data stays on your Mac. Sync goes through your own iCloud Drive.

No Chromium

Swift, SwiftUI and Apple's engines. Native apps that start fast.

Nothing extra

A few megabytes, no needless dependencies. Nothing on screen that doesn't serve.
